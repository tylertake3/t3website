4. Our approach

  • Small label above the heading: HOW WE WORK
  • Heading: Trained performers, not a novelty booking
  • Body copy: Circus reads badly on camera when it is cast on the strength of a showreel alone. We put forward performers whose training and current practice match the specific skill in the brief, we are clear about what needs rigging, matting or a qualified rigger present, and we flag it early if what has been storyboarded is not safely repeatable. If a sequence needs a specialist alongside the performer, we will say so rather than let it land on the day.

Points (3 items)

Item 1

  • Term: SKILL CONFIRMED
  • Description: The specific discipline in the brief checked against current training and practice, not a general circus credit.

Item 2

  • Term: RIG AND SPACE
  • Description: Rigging, height, matting and floor requirements raised at brief stage so the location can actually take it.

Item 3

  • Term: REPEATABLE ON THE DAY
  • Description: Cast for performers who can hold the move across takes and coverage, not land it once.
  • Pull quote — the big line set apart from the rest: “If it cannot be repeated safely on take nine, it is not a shot — it is a stunt with a different name.”

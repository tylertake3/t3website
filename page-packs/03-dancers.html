3. What we supply

  • Heading: WHAT WE SUPPLY

Genres (6 items)

Item 1

  • Name: Contemporary
  • Description: Technically trained contemporary artists for narrative and abstract movement — floor work, partnering and release-based styles, comfortable improvising to direction on set.

Item 2

  • Name: Commercial
  • Description: Sharp, camera-ready commercial dancers for promos, campaigns and live performance — fast with choreography and consistent take to take.

Item 3

  • Name: Period & historical
  • Description: Dancers drilled in court, regency, ragtime, swing and other era-specific social dance, styled and rehearsed to the production’s period.

Item 4

  • Name: Ballroom
  • Description: Competition-level ballroom and Latin couples — waltz, foxtrot, tango, quickstep and more — able to perform full routines or background social dancing.

Item 5

  • Name: Street
  • Description: Breakers, hip hop, popping, locking and krump artists from the UK battle scene, with the power moves and freestyle depth to hold a featured frame.

Item 6

  • Name: Choreographed ensemble work
  • Description: Full ensembles cast to move as one — musical numbers, ceremonies and crowd choreography, matched for height, look and partnering experience.
  • Small note: Solo artists to full ensembles, matched to the brief — style, look, height and partnering experience.

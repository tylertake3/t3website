How to fill one in

1. Open the page first — the "see the page" link above. It works with no internet connection.

2. Open your worksheet. To type into it: open it in a browser, select everything, copy, and

paste into a blank Google Doc or Word document.

3. Answer "First, the whole page" before writing anything else. Who is reading it, what

should they do next, what must they take away. Everything else gets easier after that.

4. Work down the sections. For each one: keep it, move it, or bin it — then write your

version. Saying what to cut is as useful as writing new words.

5. Finish with the missing sections. What do you explain to people over and over that the

page never mentions? That part is worth more than all the rewriting.

If you want to change how something looks rather than what it says: screenshot the page,

drop it into Canva, and move things around on top of it. Send the picture back.

Anything unclear, ask — if a question in there does not make sense, that is our fault, not yours.

## 5. Our approach

- **Small label above the heading:** HOW WE WORK
- **Heading:** Performers first, description second
- **Body copy:** Everyone on this roster is here as a professional performer. We cast them for what a brief actually needs, we ask before we describe anyone in a particular way, and we do not put people forward for work that trades on their appearance at their expense. If a role is written badly or a treatment is heading somewhere we would not be comfortable sending someone into, we will raise it with you early rather than quietly decline. It has never cost a good production anything to be asked.

### Points _(3 items)_

**Item 1**

- **Term:** REPRESENTED PROPERLY
- **Description:** Billed and briefed as performers, with the same credits, rates and conditions as any other booking.

**Item 2**

- **Term:** DESCRIBED THEIR WAY
- **Description:** How someone is described in a submission is agreed with them, not assumed by us or by a brief.

**Item 3**

- **Term:** HONEST ABOUT FIT
- **Description:** If a role is wrong for the person or the person is wrong for the role, you will hear it from us first.

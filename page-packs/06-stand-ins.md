## 3. The difference

- **Small label above the heading:** THE DIFFERENCE
- **Heading:** Six jobs, often confused. Here's what each one does.

### Cards _(6 items)_

**Item 1**

- **Number:** 01
- **Heading:** Stand-in
- **Body copy:** Takes the actor's place while shots are lit and blocked. Matched on height, build and colouring so every setup translates exactly when the actor steps in. Rarely on camera.

**Item 2**

- **Number:** 02
- **Heading:** Picture double
- **Body copy:** Appears on camera in place of the actor — over-the-shoulder angles, hands, driving shots and distance work. The match has to hold in the frame, not just under the lights.

**Item 3**

- **Number:** 03
- **Heading:** Body double
- **Body copy:** Steps in for specific physical work — physique shots, movement, anything the schedule or the script keeps the actor from doing. Cast to match in close quarters.

**Item 4**

- **Number:** 04
- **Heading:** Intimacy double
- **Body copy:** Performs intimate scenes in the actor's place, working to the intimacy coordinator's choreography — cast with consent, closed-set experience and a match that holds in frame.

**Item 5**

- **Number:** 05
- **Heading:** Hand double
- **Body copy:** Close-up insert work — writing, driving, product handling, instruments. Matched on skin tone, size and detail, steady under a macro lens.

**Item 6**

- **Number:** 06
- **Heading:** Stunt double
- **Body copy:** Takes the action the actor can't — falls, fights, vehicle work — under the stunt coordinator, matched closely enough to cut seamlessly with the performance.

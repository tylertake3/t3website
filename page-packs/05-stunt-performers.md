## 5. Our approach

- **Small label above the heading:** HOW WE WORK
- **Heading:** Safety is the schedule
- **Body copy:** Every stunt booking runs through your stunt coordinator, not around them. We supply people whose training, registration and recent experience match what the sequence actually asks for, we are straightforward about what a performer can and cannot do, and we would rather tell you a name is wrong than watch a day fall apart on the floor. Nobody on our books is put forward for action they are not cleared for.

### Points _(3 items)_

**Item 1**

- **Term:** MATCHED PROPERLY
- **Description:** Height, build, colouring and movement checked against your cast before a name is sent, not after.

**Item 2**

- **Term:** CLEARED FOR THE WORK
- **Description:** Training, registration and recent credits confirmed for the specific sequence being cast.

**Item 3**

- **Term:** COORDINATOR-LED
- **Description:** We work to your stunt coordinator's brief and keep them in the loop from first name to call sheet.

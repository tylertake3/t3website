Who we are

OUR STORY

Our team brings together experience from across performance, production and the wider creative industries, giving us an understanding of both sides of the booking process. We know what our artists need, we understand what our clients are looking for, and we know how to bring the two together.

As a close-knit team, we share knowledge, communicate quickly and adapt to the demands of an industry that rarely stands still. Every artist in our books is met by us in person, meaning our bookers know the talent they’re putting forward - their skills, their experience and, importantly, who they are.

It’s a personal approach to talent management, backed by industry knowledge and a team that knows how to get things done.

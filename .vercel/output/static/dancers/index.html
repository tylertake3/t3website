WHAT WE SUPPLY

Technically trained contemporary artists for narrative and abstract movement — floor work, partnering and release-based styles, comfortable improvising to direction on set.
Sharp, camera-ready commercial dancers for promos, campaigns and live performance — fast with choreography and consistent take to take.
Dancers drilled in court, regency, ragtime, swing and other era-specific social dance, styled and rehearsed to the production’s period.
Competition-level ballroom and Latin couples — waltz, foxtrot, tango, quickstep and more — able to perform full routines or background social dancing.
Breakers, hip hop, popping, locking and krump artists from the UK battle scene, with the power moves and freestyle depth to hold a featured frame.
Full ensembles cast to move as one — musical numbers, ceremonies and crowd choreography, matched for height, look and partnering experience.

Solo artists to full ensembles, matched to the brief — style, look, height and partnering experience.

WORKING WITH CHOREOGRAPHERS

We supply the talent. You keep the room.

Send us the brief — style, numbers, dates and look — and we shortlist from the roster, hold availability and handle contracts, fees and on-the-day coordination. You audition and direct as you always would; we take the admin off your desk.

Our commission comes from the artist’s fee, not from the client or the choreographer. Booking through Take 3 costs your production nothing extra.

When the look is the brief

WHAT WE SUPPLY

  • 01Height and stature

    Performers at the far ends of the range — from very tall to very short — cast for roles written around their height, and represented as actors rather than as a measurement.

  • 02Twins and lookalikes

    Identical and non-identical twins, close matches and doubles for continuity, split-screen, time-jump and multiple-role work.

  • 03Distinctive features

    Faces and figures that stay with an audience — striking bone structure, natural hair, tattoos, scarring, and looks a standard casting call rarely reaches.

  • 04Character and real people

    Faces with a life behind them, for productions and brands that want believable over polished, and memorable over neutral.

HOW WE WORK

Performers first, description second

Everyone on this roster is here as a professional performer. We cast them for what a brief actually needs, we ask before we describe anyone in a particular way, and we do not put people forward for work that trades on their appearance at their expense. If a role is written badly or a treatment is heading somewhere we would not be comfortable sending someone into, we will raise it with you early rather than quietly decline. It has never cost a good production anything to be asked.

REPRESENTED PROPERLY
Billed and briefed as performers, with the same credits, rates and conditions as any other booking.
DESCRIBED THEIR WAY
How someone is described in a submission is agreed with them, not assumed by us or by a brief.
HONEST ABOUT FIT
If a role is wrong for the person or the person is wrong for the role, you will hear it from us first.

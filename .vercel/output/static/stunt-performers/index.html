HOW WE WORK

Safety is the schedule

Every stunt booking runs through your stunt coordinator, not around them. We supply people whose training, registration and recent experience match what the sequence actually asks for, we are straightforward about what a performer can and cannot do, and we would rather tell you a name is wrong than watch a day fall apart on the floor. Nobody on our books is put forward for action they are not cleared for.

MATCHED PROPERLY
Height, build, colouring and movement checked against your cast before a name is sent, not after.
CLEARED FOR THE WORK
Training, registration and recent credits confirmed for the specific sequence being cast.
COORDINATOR-LED
We work to your stunt coordinator's brief and keep them in the loop from first name to call sheet.

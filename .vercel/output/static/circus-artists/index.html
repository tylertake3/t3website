HOW WE WORK

Trained performers, not a novelty booking

“If it cannot be repeated safely on take nine, it is not a shot — it is a stunt with a different name.”

Circus reads badly on camera when it is cast on the strength of a showreel alone. We put forward performers whose training and current practice match the specific skill in the brief, we are clear about what needs rigging, matting or a qualified rigger present, and we flag it early if what has been storyboarded is not safely repeatable. If a sequence needs a specialist alongside the performer, we will say so rather than let it land on the day.

SKILL CONFIRMED
The specific discipline in the brief checked against current training and practice, not a general circus credit.
RIG AND SPACE
Rigging, height, matting and floor requirements raised at brief stage so the location can actually take it.
REPEATABLE ON THE DAY
Cast for performers who can hold the move across takes and coverage, not land it once.

Same rules, different stage

Bold on camera. Careful on set.

After Dark work is booked and run the way we run every intimacy job. Boundaries are agreed in writing before the scene is blocked, choreography is rehearsed rather than improvised, and the set is closed when the material calls for it — with one point of contact for agreements, riders and fees.

AGREED IN ADVANCE
What is performed, how it is shot and how the footage may be used, set out before the shoot day.
REHEARSED
Pole, aerial and rope work is choreographed and rehearsed like a stunt — rigging checked, spotters in place, nothing left to the day.

WHAT IS A SPACT

A performer, not just a face in the crowd.

A SPACT (Special Action Extra) is a background artist with a real, proven skill: a fighter, driver, dancer, gymnast or rider who can perform that skill safely, repeatably, on camera. Productions book them where a scene needs genuine ability in the background, not a mime of it.

THE DISTINCTIONS

SPACT, Stunt Performer, Supporting Artist

The three roles sit on one spectrum of screen action, and the honest answer is that the lines are drawn by risk, pay and responsibility, not by talent.

The line between SPACT and stunt work is a judgement call on risk, and it belongs to the stunt coordinator. A good agency flags the borderline cases before they reach set; it's a conversation we have with productions weekly.
